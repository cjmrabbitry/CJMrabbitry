The Standard of Perfection, explained in plain language.

ARBA judges score every rabbit against a written standard — not against the other rabbits in the room. Each guide below breaks one breed's standard down for a total beginner, fully sourced and cross-checked against ARBA's own publications.

Worth remembering

The Standard of Perfection is a breeding and showing benchmark — not a measure of a rabbit's worth as a companion. A rabbit can be a wonderful, healthy, loving pet without meeting every point of the written standard.
